*[Polska wersja poniżej](#zastrzeżenie-medyczne).*

LightScout is **not a medical device**. It is not certified, approved or cleared by any medical or regulatory
authority, and it is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

- LightScout does **not replace** your CGM app, your insulin pump, their alarms, a blood glucose meter or the advice
  of your diabetes team. Keep the alarms of your CGM app or uploader switched on.
- LightScout shows data that other software has uploaded to your Nightscout site. That data can be late, missing,
  duplicated or wrong (for example an error code of a sensor read as a glucose value). LightScout cannot check it.
- An alarm can be late or can fail to ring: when the phone has no internet, Nightscout or the uploader is down, the
  battery saver or the phone maker's settings stop the app, the phone is off, muted, in Do not disturb, or for reasons
  not known in advance. See [known limitations](docs/limitations.md).
- Never make a treatment decision (insulin, carbohydrates, glucagon) from LightScout alone. Confirm with your CGM app
  or a blood glucose meter, and follow the plan agreed with your diabetes team.
- Before you rely on LightScout, run it in parallel with your existing alarms for several nights, and check that its
  alarms ring on your phone as you expect.
